# Model QA checklist

Every new model, prop or variant gets checked against this list before it ships. It comes straight from the
feedback on Gliddy, the plushies, Moffy and Ducky. Render it, look, fix, and render again. Never pass something
through "like it was nothing".

## Look
1. **No black outlines.** Parts meet in clean colour edges. Only eyes get thin dark rings.
2. **No colour bleeding.** Every colour stops exactly where its part ends (eye whites, tongues, ties, lids).
3. **No double outlines or black side walls** on painted shapes, from the front or the side.
4. **Colour borders follow curves** that hug the shape. No straight cuts that look "too perfect".
5. **Not just simple shapes.** Layer real details (rims, buttons, straps, stitches, highlights) so it doesn't feel fake.
6. **Reads at pet size.** Details must still show when the pet is small on screen. Make key features big enough.
7. **Original designs.** No copies of a real group's official lightstick, logo or a known artist's motifs.

## Fit and motion
8. **Nothing goes through anything.** No hand through the head, no hat sinking into the head, no item inside the body.
9. **Clothes and items follow the right body part.** Shirts follow the torso and never stretch out to the hands.
   Sleeves don't turn into wings. Held items stay in the hand.
10. **Fits every pet.** Head items sit on each pet's head (its own size and height). Held items are in proportion.
11. **Correct side and mirroring.** Left and right as designed, tips pointing the way they should.

## Checking
12. **Render from many angles** (front, both 3/4 views, both sides, back), **in poses** (idle, wave, ta-da),
    **up close** on the details, and **on several pets**.
13. **Fix every visible flaw, then re-render to confirm.** Report what's still not perfect, honestly.
