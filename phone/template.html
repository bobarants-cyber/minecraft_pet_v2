🪟 Floating Bbokari

Bbokari pops out into a little window that stays on top of your other apps, like on your Mac.

  1. Tap Float Bbokari.
  2. Go to your Home Screen or open any app. Bbokari stays in the corner.
  3. Drag him anywhere, pinch to resize, or swipe him to the side of the screen to tuck him away.
